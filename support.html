Get support

If you or someone you know needs help now.

In an emergencyCall 000 (Australia). Signs of overdose, seizure, severe confusion or suicidal intent need urgent help.
  • National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline: 1800 250 015 (connects to your state or territory service).
  • Lifeline: 13 11 14, 24 hours.
  • Your GP for withdrawal assessment and treatment.
