Three practice principles

Engage before you assess

Trust makes honest assessment possible. Start with curiosity and the person's own goals.

Screen, and flag withdrawal risk

Alcohol and benzodiazepine withdrawal can be dangerous. When in doubt, involve medical help.

Words shape care

Say "person who uses drugs", not "addict". Language affects how people are treated and whether they seek help.
