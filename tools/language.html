Language guide

Words shape care. Stigmatising language can lower the quality of care and deter people from seeking help.

Instead ofTry
Addict, junkie, alcoholicPerson who uses drugs; person with an alcohol use disorder
Substance abuseSubstance use; substance use disorder
Clean / dirty (of drug tests)Negative / positive test result
Relapse (to the person)Return to use; lapse
Non-compliantNot currently engaged; has not been able to attend
HabitUse; disorder (where diagnosed)
Drug-addicted babyBaby with neonatal withdrawal

Respect how individuals describe themselves; some people choose to identify as "an addict" or "in recovery". Follow their lead.
