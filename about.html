About and editorial policy

Purpose

Bridgework is a free learning resource on addiction medicine, addiction psychology and social work interventions.

Not clinical advice

Content is educational. It does not replace clinical supervision, local protocols, current guidelines or medical advice.

Editorial standards

  • Cite sources and note when pages were last reviewed.
  • Use person-first, non-stigmatising language.
  • Describe evidence honestly, including its limits.
  • Content has been reviewed by an addiction medicine clinician and an experienced AOD social worker.

Privacy

There are no accounts, cookies or analytics. Learning progress is stored only in your own browser.
